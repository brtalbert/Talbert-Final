About Us

Nestled in the heart of the scenic Blue Ridge Mountains, Macon County is known for its breathtaking landscapes, including waterfalls, hiking trails, and the iconic Appalachian Trail.

Founded in 1828, our county carries a proud heritage rooted in Native American history, early pioneer settlements, and a deep respect for the land. Today, Macon County offers a perfect blend of small-town charm and modern amenities, making it a wonderful place to live, work, and explore.

From the vibrant town of Franklin to the peaceful vistas of Highlands and beyond, Macon County invites you to discover a community where neighbors are like family, outdoor adventures await around every corner, and every season brings a new reason to fall in love with the mountains.

Come experience the spirit of Macon County — a place where nature, history, and hospitality are always close to home.
