Welcome to Macon County, North Carolina

Where the Mountains Embrace You

Home of Highlands • Franklin • Nantahala • Otto

Nestled in the majestic Blue Ridge Mountains, Macon County is a vibrant blend of small-town charm, natural splendor, and rich Appalachian heritage. Whether you’re here to explore our scenic byways, hike to breathtaking waterfalls, or find your place in a tight-knit community, you’ll feel right at home.

Explore Our Backyard

From the peaks of the Cowee Mountains to the peaceful flow of the Little Tennessee River, adventure awaits around every bend. Macon County is home to world-class hiking, fly fishing, gem mining, paddling, and more.

  • The Appalachian Trail: Miles of unforgettable hikes
  • Waterfalls & Vistas: Dry Falls, Whiteside Mountain and more
  • Nantahala National Forest: Endless outdoor recreation

Looking to Relocate?

Macon County offers a relaxed lifestyle with strong community values, excellent schools, local businesses, and year-round beauty. Whether you're building a family, starting a business, or enjoying retirement, you'll find opportunity here.

  • Quality healthcare and education
  • Thriving arts and cultural scene
  • Friendly towns and mountain hospitality
